Find Your LinkedIn Member ID

Step 1: Open your LinkedIn profile

Copy your profile URL from the browser address bar.

Example: https://www.linkedin.com/in/john-doe-123456789/

The number at the end (123456789) might be your Member ID.

Step 2: Use LinkedIn UGC Post Tool

This tool should display your Person URN at the top of the page.

Look for text like: urn:li:member:XXXXXXXXX or urn:li:person:XXXXXXXXX

Copy the numeric ID only (the XXXXXXXXX part).

Step 3: Enter your Member ID

Step 4: Test

After saving, restart MCP servers and test posting:

python start_mcp_servers.py
curl -X POST http://localhost:8002/create_post -H "Content-Type: application/json" -d "{\"text\": \"Test post\", \"visibility\": \"PUBLIC\"}"
